# Brand

- Product name: `Sillage`
- Positioning: second brain for notes, recall, and resurfacing
- Primary tagline: `Your second brain, resurfaced.`
- Short product description: `memory app for notes, recall, and resurfacing`

## Brand Notes

- `Sillage` is the commercial name.
- Keep `Second Brain` as a descriptor, not the primary brand.
- Tone: sober, precise, intelligent, and product-first.
- Avoid framing the app as a generic assistant or a magical AI agent.

## Alternate Taglines

- `Remember less. Recall better.`
- `Your notes, back in context.`
- `A second brain that resurfaces what matters.`
- `Turn notes into recall.`
